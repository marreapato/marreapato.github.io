My professional experience in data science informs the problems I am interested in as a researcher.

At **Outsmarting** in Brazil, I worked as a **Data Analyst** (September 2021–March 2023) and later as a **Junior Data Scientist III** (March–September 2023). My work included building dashboards using R Shiny, integrating data sources, working with SQL databases, and forecasting revenue time series with statistical and neural-network models.

I also worked on analytics instrumentation and data quality, including Google Tag Manager and the transition to Google Analytics 4. In one analytics project, efforts to improve tracking reduced the share of missing records from approximately 50% to 5–10% per day.

Earlier, during an internship at **SEPLAN** in Bahia (July–September 2021), I helped develop web applications to communicate information about public planning and government actions.

These roles strengthened my interest in systems that can make useful decisions from data. Today, my PhD research explores a related question for AI agents: how can systems retain what matters, learn from previous interactions, and adapt without repeatedly rebuilding everything they know?
