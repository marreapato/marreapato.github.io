My academic background connects **statistics**, **machine learning**, and **computer science**.

## Statistics at UFBA

I studied **Statistics at the Federal University of Bahia (UFBA)** in Brazil, completing my bachelor's degree in **December 2022**. This gave me a foundation in statistical modeling, data analysis, and working with real-world data.

## Master's at UFPE

From **August 2023 to July 2025**, I completed a **Master's degree in Computer Science** at the **Centro de Informática, Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE)**. My work focused on machine learning and data mining, including research on **explainable AI**.

## PhD at the University of Galway

In **September 2025**, I began a **PhD in Computer Science** at the **University of Galway** in Ireland. My current research interests include **AI agents, memory-augmented learning, continual learning, and inference-time adaptation**. I am interested in how agents can use external memory to retain useful experiences and knowledge while adapting to changing tasks and information.

## Connecting research and practice

Before starting my PhD, I worked with data analytics and data science at **Outsmarting**, including time-series forecasting, data integration, and analytics. These experiences continue to motivate my interest in practical, resource-aware AI systems.
