On **30 September–1 October 2026**, I volunteered as a host at the **European Big Data Value Forum (EBDVF)** in Galway, Ireland. The event brought together researchers, companies and public-sector participants working on data-driven innovation and artificial intelligence across Europe.

As a PhD student interested in **AI agents, memory and continual learning**, I valued the opportunity to learn more about **data spaces**, their role in data sharing and interoperability, and how this work connects to AI research. Supporting the event also gave me a chance to interact with people from different organizations and countries.

I am grateful to **Prof. Ed Curry**, **RinnAI**, and the **University of Galway** for their support and the opportunity to take part.

This experience was a reminder that research also develops through community engagement: listening to practitioners, understanding their challenges and connecting technical work with real applications.

[Learn more about EBDVF](https://european-big-data-value-forum.eu/).
